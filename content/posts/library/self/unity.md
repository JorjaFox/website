UNITY is a 2015 documentary film written, directed and produced by Shaun Monson, and is the sequel to the 2005 film EARTHLINGS.  Like its predecessor, the film is presented in chapters, (Cosmic, Mind, Body, Heart and Soul) but this time the documentary is voiced by an astounding cast of 100 celebrity narrators.  UNITY is now available on iTunes, Amazon, Microsoft, Google Play, Vimeo On Demand, Vudu and M-Go.

Jorja is one of the movie's narrators.
