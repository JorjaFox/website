It's been a rough time since October. The good news is I got a new job. The bad news... Well. You can see what's going on in the US.

But I am happy to say that after some elbow grease, legal meetings, and luck, there are image galleries for TV shows again! They are only the approved publicity images I have legal access to, no fan submissions. Fingers crossed things don't change again, but if they do, it's easy enough to disable.
