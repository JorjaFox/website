Since 1888, Five Acres has been supporting children and families within their communities. They partner with families across Southern California to create a permanent, safe, and healthy environment for every child.

They offer a full range of behavioral health services at each of our many clinics, via telehealth, or by coming to people directly. They also offer comprehensive inpatient care for children who need 24/7 care.
